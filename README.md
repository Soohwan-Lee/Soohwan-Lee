## Research Interests

My work sits at the intersection of HCI, CSCW, and social psychology. I build AI systems that participate in group processes, run controlled experiments with them, and examine how social influence, power imbalance, and dissent play out when AI is in the loop.

- Group-centered AI and group experience (GX)
- Minority influence and dissent in AI-mediated groups
- Multi-agent LLM systems as social actors
- AI proxies and mediation in negotiation
- Reflective decision support for teams
